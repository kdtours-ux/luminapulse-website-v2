About

About the Director

Dustin Chien

I'm Dustin Chien, founder of Lumina Pulse Motion Pictures, a filmmaker based in Taipei.

I make commercials, documentaries, music videos and automotive films. I care about beautiful images, but more importantly, why we shoot something a certain way and what we want the audience to feel.

To me, every project is a film. Find the story, find the way it should look and feel, and make it properly.
